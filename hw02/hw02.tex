\section*{Evaluation Contexts}
Consider the grammar from homework 01, with $+,-,*$ shortened to $\oplus$:
\[
  \begin{array}{l c r r}
    \oplus & \coloneq & + \mid - \mid * \mid \vee \mid \wedge & \emph{Binary Operations}\\
    t & \coloneq & n \mid \mathtt{true} \mid \mathtt{false} \mid t \oplus t  \mid \mathtt{if}(t;t,t) & \emph{Terms}\\
  \end{array}
\]
We define the following operational semantics for this language

\begin{align*}
  v & \coloneq n \mid \mathtt{true} \mid \mathtt{false} & \emph{Values}\\
  n_1 \oplus n_2 & \rightarrow & (v_1\oplus v_2) \text{ for } \oplus \in \{+,-,*\}\\
  \mathtt{if}(\texttt{true};t_1,t_2) & \rightarrow & t_1\\
  \mathtt{if}(\texttt{false};t_1,t_2) & \rightarrow & t_2 \\
  \mathtt{true} \vee \mathtt{true} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{true} \\
  \mathtt{true} \vee \mathtt{false} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{true} \\
  \mathtt{false} \vee \mathtt{true} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{true} \\ 
  \mathtt{false} \vee \mathtt{false} &\rightarrow & \mathtt{false} \\ 
  \mathtt{true} \wedge \mathtt{true} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{true}\\
  \mathtt{true} \wedge \mathtt{false} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{false}\\
  \mathtt{false} \wedge \mathtt{true} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{false}\\
  \mathtt{false} \wedge \mathtt{false} & \rightarrow & \mathtt{false} 
\end{align*}

\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
  \begin{minipage}{0.3\textwidth}
    \begin{prooftree}
      \AxiomC{$t_1\rightarrow t_1^{\prime}$}
      \UnaryInfC{$t_1\oplus t_2 \rightarrow t_1^{\prime}\oplus t_2$}
    \end{prooftree}
  \end{minipage}
  \hfill
  \begin{minipage}{0.3\textwidth}
    \begin{prooftree}
      \AxiomC{$t_2\rightarrow t_2^{\prime}$}
      \UnaryInfC{$v \oplus t_2 \rightarrow v \oplus t_2^{\prime}$}
    \end{prooftree}
  \end{minipage}
  \hfill
  \begin{minipage}{0.3\textwidth}
    \begin{prooftree}
      \AxiomC{$t_1\rightarrow t_1^{\prime}$}
      \UnaryInfC{$\mathtt{ifz}(t_1;t_2,t_3) \rightarrow \mathtt{ifz}(t_1^{\prime};t_2,t_3)$}
    \end{prooftree}
  \end{minipage}
  \hfill
  \vspace{2em}
\end{minipage}

Here $(v_1 + v_2)$ means we take the integer given by the sum of values $v_1$ and $v_2$ (instead of the term $v_1+v_2$, and similarly for $-$ and $*$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Which of the above rules are congruence rules? Which are computation rules?
  \item Re-formulate the congruence rules using \emph{evaluation contexts}.
    That is, write an abstract grammar for $E$ such that all congruence rules above can be replaced with the single rule:
\end{itemize}

\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
  \begin{prooftree}
    \AxiomC{$t\rightarrow t^{\prime}$}
    \UnaryInfC{$E[t] \rightarrow E[t^{\prime}]$}
  \end{prooftree}
  \hfill
  \vspace{1em}
\end{minipage}

Here, $E[t]$ denotes the result of ``plugging in'' $t$ in $E$, i.e., replacing $\square$ with $t$ in $E$.

\subsection*{Induction on Derivation Trees}
Take the definition of $\mathtt{size}$ from the last homework, expanded for the new language:
\[
\begin{array}{l c r}
\mathtt{size}(n) & \coloneq&  1\\
  \mathtt{size}(\mathtt{true}) & \coloneq & 1\\
  \mathtt{size}(\mathtt{false}) & \coloneq & 1\\
\mathtt{size}(t_1 \oplus t_2) &\coloneq& \mathtt{size}(t_1)+\mathtt{size}(t_2) + 1\\
\mathtt{size}(\mathtt{if(t_1;t_2,t_3)}) &\coloneq& \mathtt{size}(t_1)+\mathtt{size}(t_2)+\mathtt{size}(t_3) + 1
\end{array}
\]

Using induction on derivation trees, show that for any two terms $t,t^{\prime}$ with $t\mapsto t^{\prime}$ we have $\mathtt{size}(t) \geq \mathtt{size}(t^{\prime})$.
