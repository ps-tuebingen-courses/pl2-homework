\subsection*{CBV}
\begin{align*}
  ((\lambda f.\lambda v.v\ v)\ ((\lambda x.x)\ (\lambda y.\lambda z.y)))\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\ 
  ((\lambda f.\lambda v.v\ v)\ (\lambda y.\lambda z.y))\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto\\
  (\lambda v.v\ v)\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\ 
  (\lambda v.v\ v)\ ((\lambda y.y)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\
  (\lambda v.v\ v)\ (\lambda y.y) & \mapsto\\
  (\lambda y.y)\ (\lambda y.y) & \mapsto \\
  \lambda y.y
\end{align*}

\subsection*{CBN}
\begin{align*}
  ((\lambda f.\lambda v.v\ v)\ ((\lambda x.x)\ (\lambda y.\lambda z.y)))\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\ 
  (\lambda v.v\ v)\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\
  ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y))\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\ 
  ((\lambda y.y)\ (\lambda y.y))\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y))& \mapsto \\
  (\lambda y.y)\ ((\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y)) & \mapsto \\
  (\lambda a.a\ a)\ (\lambda y.y) & \mapsto\\ 
  (\lambda y.y)\ (\lambda y.y) & \mapsto \\
  \lambda y.y
\end{align*}

Call-by-name might evaluate arguments multiple times, if they are used multiple times in the body, but not at all if they are not used in the body.
Call-by-value always evaluates each argument to a function exactly once. 
This is especially important when considering languages with side effects.
